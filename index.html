Merchant Brain

A Free Market price tracker for MapleStory Classic.

Merchant Brain is a Windows desktop application. While you browse Free Market shops the way you normally would, it reads each shop window off your screen, records what is listed and what has sold, learns what items actually trade for, and tells you when something is priced well below its going rate.

It only reads your screen. Merchant Brain does not play the game for you. It does not modify game files, read game memory, intercept network traffic, or send any input to the game. You open the shops, and you buy and sell yourself.

What it does

  • Alerts while you browse. Every listing is checked as it appears on screen. When something is well under what it has been selling for, you get a sound and a notice showing the item, the price, your profit after the 3% shop fee, and where the shop is.
  • Four kinds of alert - cheap against recent sales, cheap against the last week, cheap against what other sellers are asking, and items selling below what an NPC pays.
  • A watchlist for specific items you are hunting, with an optional maximum price.
  • Prices built from observed sales, weighted so recent ones count for more. Obvious nonsense prices are discarded, and equipment is flagged rather than priced, because individual stats vary.
  • Price checks and listing guidance for items you want to sell.
  • A profit ledger recording what you actually made.
  • Everything stays on your computer. Your scan history is stored locally and is not uploaded.
