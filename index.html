Merchant Brain

A Free Market price tracker for MapleStory Classic.

Merchant Brain is a Windows desktop application for players who buy and resell items in the Free Market. While you browse shops the way you normally would, it reads each shop window off your screen, records what is listed and what has sold, learns what items actually trade for, and tells you the moment something is priced well below its going rate.

It only reads your screen. Merchant Brain does not play the game for you. It does not modify game files, read game memory, intercept network traffic, or send any input to the game. You open the shops, and you buy and sell yourself. It is a notebook that reads faster than you do.

What it does

Tells you when something is underpriced

Every listing is checked the moment it appears on your screen. When an item is well under what it has been selling for, you get a sound and an on-screen notice with the item, its price, your profit after the 3% shop fee, the price to relist it at, and which channel, room and shop it is in. The alert reaches you even while the game is the active window.

Four kinds of alert

  • Clearly underpriced — cheap measured against real recent sales.
  • Possibly underpriced — few sales recently, but the last week supports it. Flagged as the weaker signal it is.
  • Below the asking market — no sales data needed: other sellers consistently list it far higher.
  • Below NPC value — priced under what a shop NPC will pay you for it.

Prices built from observed sales, not guesses

Values come from sales Merchant Brain actually watched happen, weighted so recent ones count for more — a price that moves does not leave you quoting last week's number. Obvious nonsense listings are discarded automatically, and nothing is ever valued above what an NPC charges for it. Equipment is flagged rather than priced, because two copies of the same item can be worth very different amounts depending on their stats.

Watchlist

Add the items you are hunting, with an optional maximum price. Partial names work, so one entry can cover a whole family of items. You are told when one appears, including items you have never scanned before.

Selling, not just buying

Price-check any item to see what it has sold for over several time windows, what the cheapest copy currently listed is, and what to price yours at if you want it gone quickly.

A record of whether it is working

A ledger tracks what you actually bought and sold, your real profit, how long items sat before selling, and which items have been worth your time. It also reports how well its own alerts have performed, so you can tighten or loosen them on evidence rather than on feel.

It asks rather than guesses

If a tooltip covers part of a shop window, or an item name has not been seen before, that row goes into a list for you to confirm instead of being written into your data as a guess. Wrong data is worse than missing data.

Questions

Does it play the game for me?

No. It reads your screen and nothing else. You open the shops, and you buy and sell yourself. It sends no input to the game at any point.

Is it safe to use?

We will not make claims about what any game operator does or does not permit, and we would be suspicious of anyone who does. What we can tell you precisely is what the software does: it reads pixels already displayed on your own screen, and it writes nothing to the game, its files, its memory or its network traffic. Read that and decide for yourself.

Does it work at my resolution?

Anything from 1280×720 to 4K, windowed or full screen. It measures your screen itself rather than relying on fixed positions, so there is nothing to set up. Larger game windows read most reliably.

What happens when I first run it?

It starts empty. It has no prices until you scan shops, and it becomes more useful the more of the market you have walked past.

Can I use it on two computers?

One key covers one computer. Moving to a new machine is free — open a ticket with the code from the new computer and your key is transferred.

What if it does not work on my PC?

Open a ticket and we will try to get it working. If we cannot, you are refunded in full within the window below.

Do I lose my data when there is an update?

No. Your history and settings are kept. You run the new version and carry on.

Privacy

What Merchant Brain records stays on your computer. The shops you scan, the prices it learns and the profit ledger are stored in a folder on your own machine. None of it is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

The only thing the application sends over the internet is a periodic check that your licence is valid. That check involves your licence key and the code identifying your computer, and nothing about your gameplay or your scans.

We hold what you would expect us to hold in order to sell and support software: your Discord account name, your licence key, the computer code it is locked to, and a record of your payment. We do not sell it, share it or use it for anything other than supporting you. Ask us to delete your records and we will, once your licence has ended.
